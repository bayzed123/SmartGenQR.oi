Frequently Asked Questions

What is SmartGen Tools? ▼
SmartGen is an all-in-one digital utility platform providing 131+ free, privacy-first web tools for developers, marketers, and SEO professionals. All our tools run locally in your browser, meaning your data never leaves your device.
Is SmartGen really free to use? ▼
Yes! SmartGen is 100% free to use. We believe in providing high-quality digital utilities to everyone without any hidden costs or subscriptions.
How does SmartGen protect my privacy? ▼
SmartGen tools are built using client-side technology. This means all processing happens directly in your web browser. We do not store, track, or even see the data you input into our tools. Zero server logs, zero data tracking.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
No account is required. You can access and use all our tools immediately without any registration or login process.
How often are new tools added? ▼
We regularly update our platform and add new tools based on user feedback and industry trends. You can check our Changelog for the latest updates.
Can I request a specific tool? ▼
Absolutely! We love hearing from our users. You can use our Contact Page to request a new tool or suggest improvements to existing ones.
Is the code for SmartGen open source? ▼
Yes, our engine is open source. You can find our repository on GitHub and even contribute to the project if you're a developer.
