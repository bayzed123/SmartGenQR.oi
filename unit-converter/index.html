Common Conversion Reference Points

A few conversions that come up constantly and are worth knowing offhand:

1. Length

1 meter ≈ 3.28 feet; 1 mile ≈ 1.61 kilometers; 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters exactly — useful for anything from shipping dimensions to recipe measurements.

2. Weight

1 kilogram ≈ 2.2 pounds; 1 pound = 16 ounces — common when reading product specs or nutrition labels from a region using a different system.

3. Temperature

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Unlike length and weight, temperature conversion isn't a simple multiplication — this tool handles the formula for you.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How accurate are the conversions?

A: The tool uses standard, internationally recognized conversion factors (e.g. 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly), so results are as precise as the input value you provide.

Q: Which unit categories are supported?

A: Length (meters, kilometers, centimeters, feet, inches, miles, yards, and more), weight, and temperature — each with its own tab in the tool.

Q: Can I convert negative or decimal values?

A: Yes — decimals work normally, and negative values are accepted where they make physical sense (temperature, for instance).
