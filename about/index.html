Who We Are

SmartGen is a free, browser-based platform offering more than 40 utilities for developers, SEO professionals, digital marketers, content creators, students, business owners, and everyday internet users. Every tool — from a JSON formatter to a UTM link builder — is designed to open instantly, run without an account, and keep your data on your own device rather than a remote server.

SmartGen isn't a venture-funded startup working toward a future subscription model. It's an independent, founder-led project, built and maintained because the small utilities professionals rely on every day deserve to be fast, honest, and free of friction.

Our Story

SmartGen started as a personal frustration. Everyday tasks — decoding a Base64 string, validating a JSON payload, building a tracking link, compressing an image before uploading it — kept leading to the same dead ends: tools locked behind account walls, files uploaded to servers with no clear privacy policy, and interfaces cluttered with ads styled to look like download buttons.

Rather than accept that as the standard, SmartGen was built as a client-side alternative from the start: a single platform where each tool processes data locally in the browser, loads without waiting on a distant server, and stays free because there was never a plan to charge for it.

What began as a small set of utilities built to support a digital marketing agency's own workflow has grown into a broader suite spanning development, SEO, marketing, and everyday calculations — added one tool at a time, and maintained the same way.

Our Mission

SmartGen's mission is to make professional-grade web utilities accessible to anyone, without asking them to pay for access, hand over an email address, or send their data to a server they've never heard of.

We measure success by a simple question: did this tool solve your problem faster than searching for, signing up for, and learning a heavier alternative would have? If a utility on this site doesn't clear that bar, we consider it unfinished, not shipped.

Privacy Commitment

Privacy at SmartGen isn't a policy statement added after the fact — it's a technical constraint the tools are designed around. Wherever the underlying task allows it, processing happens locally in your browser using standard JavaScript and Web APIs. When you format JSON, extract colors from an image, calculate a loan payment, or generate a password, that data is handled on your device and is never transmitted to a SmartGen server.

Because of that, we don't require an account — there's nothing to attach one to. We don't ask for an email address to unlock a tool, and we don't store your inputs in a database, because in the large majority of cases, we never receive them in the first place.

A small number of tools necessarily involve checking a public data source — an IP or domain lookup, for example. Even there, only the specific input that task needs is used, and it isn't connected to any account or profile, because none exists on SmartGen.

Editorial Standards

Every article, guide, and explanation published on SmartGen — including this page — is written and personally reviewed by our founder, Sayad Md Bayezid Hosan. We do not publish AI-generated articles, and content on this site is never machine-written and posted without human authorship.

Where AI tools are used at all, their role is limited to engineering support: assisting with debugging, brainstorming implementation approaches, and technical research during development. That's a coding aid, not an editorial one, and it doesn't extend to the words you read on any SmartGen page.

We hold this distinction deliberately. A tool's documentation is only useful if it reflects real use of that tool, and a mission statement is only honest if a person actually means it. Both are true here.

Quality Assurance

Before a tool ships — and on an ongoing basis afterward — it goes through the same quality process:

  • Cross-browser testing across Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge
  • Performance checks to keep load times and processing fast on modest hardware
  • Accessibility passes for keyboard navigation, screen readers, and color contrast
  • Responsive testing across phone, tablet, and desktop breakpoints
  • Usability review of every input, label, and error message
  • Scheduled maintenance to catch breaking changes in browser APIs or dependencies

This isn't a one-time launch checklist. Browsers update, standards shift, and a tool that worked perfectly last year can quietly break. Revisiting existing tools is treated with the same priority as building new ones.

Technical Philosophy

SmartGen is built on modern HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript — deliberately without a heavy framework stack for most tools, because the fastest code to load is code that was never shipped in the first place.

We follow a progressive enhancement approach: a tool's core layout and content are usable immediately, with interactive behavior layered on top rather than required just to render the page. Combined with a client-side-first architecture, this keeps most tools usable the moment they load, without waiting on round trips to a server.

This is also why the site looks and behaves consistently from page to page. Every tool shares the same underlying design system and JavaScript foundation, so what you learn using one utility — how to copy a result, how settings are laid out — carries over to the next.

Ethical Advertising

SmartGen is free to use because it's supported by display advertising, primarily through Google AdSense. We consider that a trade worth being transparent about, and we hold our own advertising to a higher bar than the minimum required.

  • Ads follow the Better Ads Standards and Google's AdSense program policies
  • No fake or disguised "download" buttons, and no ads styled to look like tool controls
  • No pop-unders, no auto-playing audio, and no interstitials that block a tool you came to use
  • Ad placements are fixed in the layout, not injected in ways that shift content while you're reading or interacting with it

If an ad on SmartGen ever violates this standard, that's a bug, not a business decision — and we treat it that way when it's reported.

Accessibility Commitment

Accessibility is treated as a requirement, not an enhancement. Practically, that means:

  • Semantic HTML structure, so screen readers and assistive technology can navigate the page logically
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds, including in dark mode
  • Interactive elements that are reachable and operable by keyboard alone
  • Descriptive labels and alt text instead of layout-only cues like color or icon shape
  • Text that resizes cleanly without breaking layout, for users who increase browser font size

We don't claim perfect compliance with every WCAG success criterion on every page — that would be an easy claim to make and a hard one to verify. What we can commit to is that accessibility is checked as part of our quality process, not treated as an afterthought, and that reported issues get fixed.

Security Commitment

SmartGen is served exclusively over HTTPS, with a Content Security Policy and standard protective headers — including X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, and X-XSS-Protection — applied site-wide to reduce common attack surfaces like clickjacking and MIME-type sniffing.

Because most tools process data client-side and no account system exists, there is simply less sensitive data for a breach to expose in the first place. That's a deliberate side effect of the privacy architecture described above, not a separate security feature.

Dependencies, browser compatibility, and headers are reviewed on an ongoing basis rather than left untouched after launch. If you believe you've found a security issue, we want to hear about it before anyone else does.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SmartGen really free to use?

Yes. Every tool on SmartGen is free, with no premium tier, credit card, or usage limit attached. The site is supported by advertising, not subscriptions.

Do I need to create an account?

No. None of SmartGen's tools require registration, a login, or an email address.

Is my data uploaded to your servers when I use a tool?

For the large majority of tools, no — processing happens locally in your browser. A small number of tools that check public data, such as a domain lookup, send only the specific input that task requires.

Who builds and maintains SmartGen?

SmartGen is an independent, founder-led project built and maintained by Sayad Md Bayezid Hosan, founder of Connect With Bayezid (CWB Agency).

Do you use AI to write your articles?

No. Every article and guide on SmartGen is written and personally reviewed by our founder. AI tools are used only for development support, such as debugging and technical research — never for writing published content.

How does SmartGen make money if everything is free?

Through display advertising, primarily Google AdSense, shown under the Better Ads Standards. SmartGen does not run pop-unders, disguised download buttons, or content-blocking interstitials.

Will SmartGen ever charge for tools that are free today?

There are no plans to introduce a paid tier. SmartGen was built to stay free, and its architecture — no accounts, minimal server processing — keeps it inexpensive to run that way.

Is SmartGen accessible for users with disabilities?

Accessibility is part of the standard quality process: semantic HTML, keyboard operability, and color contrast are checked on every tool. Report any issue you find and it will be fixed.

How often are tools updated?

Continuously. Existing tools are revisited when browser standards change or issues are reported, and new tools are added based on user requests.

How do I report a bug or request a new tool?

Use the contact link in the site footer. Include the tool name, your browser, and what you expected versus what actually happened.
