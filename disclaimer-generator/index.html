What Is a Website Disclaimer?

A disclaimer is a legal notice that limits your liability for the content, advice, or products on your site — covering things like "results may vary," affiliate relationships, or "not professional medical/financial advice." It's distinct from a Privacy Policy (which covers data handling) and Terms & Conditions (which cover usage rules).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is a disclaimer legally required?

A: Not universally, but it's strongly recommended for any site giving advice, reviews, or earning affiliate income — it's a key piece of evidence that you clearly communicated limitations to visitors.

Q: What's the difference between a disclaimer and a privacy policy?

A: A disclaimer limits liability for your content and advice. A privacy policy explains what personal data you collect and how it's used — they cover completely different legal ground and most sites need both.

Q: Can I edit the generated text?

A: Yes — the output is plain HTML, so you can adjust wording, add clauses specific to your business, or restyle it to match your site before publishing.
