What Makes This Notepad "Secure"?

Most "online notepad" tools save your text to a remote server — this one doesn't. Your notes are saved to your browser's local storage on your own device and auto-save as you type, but nothing is ever transmitted anywhere. Close the tab, come back later, and your notes are still there — because they never left your machine in the first place.

Useful for a quick scratch pad, draft text, or anything you'd rather not put through a cloud-synced notes app.

How to Use the Secure Notepad

  1. Start typing — your text saves to local storage automatically as you go.
  2. Close the tab anytime — your notes persist and will still be there next time you open the page in the same browser.
  3. Clear it manually when you're done, using the clear option — notes don't expire on their own.

Understanding the Privacy Trade-Offs

1. Local storage is device- and browser-specific

Because nothing is synced to a server, your notes only exist in the browser and device you wrote them in — they won't appear if you open this page on a different computer or a different browser.

2. Clearing browser data will erase your notes

Since this uses the browser's local storage, clearing your browser's site data/cache for SmartGen will delete saved notes — copy out anything important before doing that.

3. It's a scratchpad, not encrypted long-term storage

This is ideal for quick, temporary, private notes you don't want touching a server — for anything you need backed up or synced across devices, use a dedicated notes app instead.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Where exactly are my notes stored?

A: In your browser's local storage on your own device — not on SmartGen's servers, not in the cloud, and not synced anywhere.

Q: Will my notes sync across devices?

A: No — local storage is specific to one browser on one device. Opening this page on your phone won't show notes written on your laptop.

Q: What happens if I clear my browser cache?

A: Clearing site data/cookies for smartgentools.com will erase saved notes, since they live in that same local storage. Copy anything important elsewhere first.
