What Does a Hashtag Generator Do?

A hashtag generator takes a topic or keyword and suggests related hashtags to widen your post's reach on Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter — beyond the handful you'd think of on your own. More relevant hashtags means more chances of a post surfacing in hashtag search and explore feeds.

How to Generate Hashtags

  1. Enter a keyword or topic describing your post — e.g. "travel," "fitness," "smallbusiness."
  2. Review the generated hashtags — a mix of broad and specific tags related to your topic.
  3. Copy and paste the set into your caption or the first comment.

Using Hashtags Effectively

1. Mix broad and niche tags

A hashtag with millions of posts gets your content buried fast; a smaller, specific niche tag gives a longer window of visibility. Use a blend of both.

2. Relevance matters more than volume

Platforms increasingly penalize hashtags unrelated to your actual content — pick tags that genuinely describe your post, not just the highest-traffic ones you can find.

3. Check each platform's hashtag limit

Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post but often performs best with far fewer (5-15) well-chosen ones; X/Twitter is more effective with 1-3.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many hashtags should I actually use?

A: It varies by platform — Instagram tolerates up to 30 but often performs better with a focused 5-15; X/Twitter and LinkedIn work best with just 1-3 highly relevant tags.

Q: Do more hashtags always mean more reach?

A: No — irrelevant or spammy-looking hashtag stuffing can actually reduce reach on some platforms. Relevance and a mix of tag sizes beats sheer quantity.

Q: Should hashtags go in the caption or the first comment?

A: Both work on Instagram — putting them in the first comment keeps your caption cleaner, while putting them in the caption itself is slightly more reliable for indexing. Either is fine.
