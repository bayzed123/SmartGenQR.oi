1. Privacy Overview

SmartGen ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection when you use our website and tools.

Key Principle: SmartGen operates on a client-side processing model. This means the vast majority of data processing happens directly in your web browser, not on our servers. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to download or share it.

2. What Data We Collect (and Don't Collect)

Data We DO NOT Collect:

  • Personal Information: We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information
  • Tool Input Data: The data you input into our tools (URLs, text, code, images, etc.) is processed entirely in your browser and never sent to our servers
  • Financial Information: We don't collect credit card numbers, bank details, or payment information (all tools are free)
  • Sensitive Data: We don't collect passwords, API keys, or other sensitive credentials
  • User Profiles: We don't require account creation or maintain user profiles

Data We MAY Collect (Minimal):

  • Server Logs: Standard web server logs including IP address, browser type, and pages visited (for security and performance monitoring)
  • Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated analytics through Google Analytics (can be disabled via browser settings)
  • Contact Form Data: If you contact us, we collect the information you provide (email, message) to respond to your inquiry
  • Cookies: Minimal cookies for functionality (theme preference, session management)

Why This Matters

Unlike most online tools, SmartGen doesn't require you to upload your data to external servers. Your sensitive information—code, images, personal data—stays on your device. This is a fundamental security advantage that protects you from data breaches and unauthorized access.

3. How We Process Your Data

Client-Side Processing (Your Device):

Most SmartGen tools process data entirely in your browser using JavaScript. This means:

  • Data never travels to our servers
  • Processing happens instantly on your device
  • Your data remains completely private
  • Tools work offline after initial page load
  • No risk of server-side data breaches affecting your information

Server-Side Processing (When Necessary):

Some advanced features may require server-side processing (e.g., image hosting, file uploads). When this occurs:

  • We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Data is processed immediately and not stored permanently
  • Temporary files are automatically deleted after processing
  • We don't share data with third parties
  • You have full control over what you upload

4. Cookies & Tracking

Essential Cookies:

  • Theme Preference: Remembers your light/dark mode choice
  • Session Management: Maintains your session for security
  • Functionality: Enables core website features

Analytics Cookies:

We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with SmartGen. This helps us improve the platform. You can disable analytics tracking through:

  • Browser settings (Do Not Track)
  • Google Analytics opt-out browser extension
  • Your browser's cookie management settings

No Third-Party Tracking:

We don't use third-party tracking pixels, retargeting cookies, or behavioral advertising trackers. Your browsing behavior is not sold to advertisers.

5. Third-Party Services

Services We Use:

  • Google Analytics: For anonymous, aggregated usage statistics
  • Google AdSense: For displaying non-intrusive advertisements
  • Google Fonts: For typography (no personal data collected)
  • CDN Services: For fast content delivery (no personal data stored)

Data Sharing:

We do NOT share your personal data with third parties. The services listed above operate under their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies if you have concerns.

6. Data Security

Security Measures:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
  • Content Security Policy: Prevents unauthorized code injection and XSS attacks
  • Security Headers: X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, and other protective headers
  • Regular Updates: Security patches applied promptly
  • No Password Storage: Since we don't collect passwords, there's nothing to breach

Security Advantage of Client-Side Processing

Because most data processing happens in your browser, there's no centralized database of your information that could be hacked. This is inherently more secure than server-side processing.

7. Your Rights

GDPR Rights (for EU Users):

  • Right to Access: Request what data we have about you
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt-out of data collection

CCPA Rights (for California Users):

  • Right to Know: Know what personal data is collected
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal data
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: No discrimination for exercising rights

How to Exercise Your Rights:

Contact us at privacy@smartgentools.com with your request. We'll respond within 30 days.
