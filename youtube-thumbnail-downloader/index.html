Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why isn't the full HD (maxresdefault) thumbnail available for every video?

A: YouTube only generates the highest resolution thumbnail if the uploader's source video meets a minimum resolution — older or lower-quality uploads may only have smaller sizes available.

Q: Is downloading a thumbnail legal?

A: Thumbnails are publicly hosted images, but the underlying artwork can still be copyrighted by the creator — downloading for personal reference or research is generally fine, but reusing it commercially without permission is not.

Q: Does this work for private or unlisted videos?

A: It works for any video whose thumbnail is publicly accessible at YouTube's standard thumbnail URLs — private videos generally won't resolve since their thumbnails aren't publicly served.
