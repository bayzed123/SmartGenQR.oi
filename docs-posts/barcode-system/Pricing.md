# Pricing

## Current offer

**SmartGen Barcode Service is presented as a $10 lifetime service.**

The current product focus is the reliable first-party browser tool: local barcode validation, local SVG generation, and the client-admin product-form integration. Checkout, PayPal, local payment collection, automatic activation, and online license verification are intentionally **not enabled yet**.

## What the current service covers

| Included | Not currently enabled |
|---|---|
| SmartGen-owned browser validation | Checkout or payment processing |
| First-party SVG barcode rendering | PayPal or local payment configuration |
| Supported format rules | Automatic account activation |
| Client product-form integration | SmartGen-hosted client product storage |
| Documentation and test guidance | Client secret-key management |

## Why payment is deferred

The tool must remain stable before a payment workflow is introduced. Separating barcode generation from payment collection avoids making the client’s product workflow dependent on a payment provider or a new SmartGen product-data service.

When SmartGen is ready to activate payments, the payment design should preserve the existing privacy boundary: client product data remains with the client, and only the minimum information necessary for payment or license management is handled by the future SmartGen-owned payment system.
